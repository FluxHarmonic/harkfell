How Harkfell was made

I designed Harkfell and I direct every part of how it's made. AI agents
write the code, the rooms, the pixel art and the music from my direction,
and I play every build, listen to every sound, and decide what stays. No
image generator was used: an agent wrote the code that draws the pixel art,
pixel for pixel. If the use of AI is a dealbreaker for you, I understand!
The source and the commit history are public, so you can see exactly how it
was made: https://github.com/FluxHarmonic/harkfell

David Wilson, Flux Harmonic
