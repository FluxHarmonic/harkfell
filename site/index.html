Harkfell

A quiet little platformer about listening.

The stones on the fells used to ring, and now only the wind does. Wander, climb, and stand still long enough to hear where the missing notes went.

Every note you bring home changes how the whole world sounds, and every so often, in a room you've never seen, the world plays you a song!

Harkfell is a game of listening. Headphones bring it closest.

Play Free, in your browser, with a keyboard or a touch screen.

This is an early slice of Harkfell: the Hollow and Reedfen, the first two regions of the world. Everything you hear is synthesized live while you play, so no two walks through it sound quite the same.

Made by David Wilson at Flux Harmonic. Written in Sigil.
