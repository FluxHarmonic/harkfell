Harkfell 0.4 takes you up for the first time: Cloudstair, a region of cliffs and open sky above the Hollow!

## Before you climb, go back to the reeds

If you've been playing since 0.3, there's something in Reedfen you missed, because it wasn't there yet. Head back to the reeds with the amber note and look behind the stone. What you find there changes how you fall, and you'll need it up high. Your 0.3 save carries over.

## Cloudstair

Past the blue stone at the Hollow's eastern edge, the fell breaks into pale cliffs and wide shelves, with slow clouds drifting behind the stone. You can't just climb these walls. The way up is the air itself: narrow streams of rising wind, and the small tufted seeds that ride them.

The wind doesn't always help. Every few seconds a gust sweeps across the open gaps. You'll hear it coming a moment before it arrives, and once you learn its rhythm, riding it makes the long jumps much easier. Some of the rising streams only lift you while it blows. It's 22 rooms, which brings the world to 104.

Listen for a rose note from somewhere high in the east. Some routes carry you away from its sound before they bring you back toward it. The seeds keep their distance at first and only answer once you've found what they're listening for.

When you've found it, don't worry about the long way down. Cloudstair has a way of seeing you home.

## Also in this release

- When a piece of music starts, the sky now softens toward the haze and clears again when the music ends. Before, a shader could turn the sky's color bands into jagged stripes that looked like corrupted textures.
- The notches on the stone gates now sit centered in the stone. Gates with more notches drifted further to the right.
- Picking up a note now plays its tune the moment you pick it up. Before, a tune could arrive late, or not at all, if another piece of music had just started.
- The Pipes' pickup, snail and seep voices are clearer on small speakers like phones and laptops.
