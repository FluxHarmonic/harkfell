Harkfell 0.4 opens Cloudstair, a new region above the Hollow with 22 rooms of pale cliffs, dry grass and slow clouds. The world now has 104 rooms.

## Into the wind

Bring the blue note home from the Pipes and look for the sheltered updraft above the Hollow's eastern edge. A tuft of down lets you glide when you hold jump, and the rising air can carry you higher.

The wind has a rhythm. Watch the seeds and listen for the breath before a gust. There are broad shelves to wait on, two gaps to cross with the wind, and a rose note calling from the high eastern crag.

When you find it, the seeds answer. Bring it through the Windworn Arch and the homeward wind carries your glide down toward the launch column and the Hollow. The rose stone under the bowl has a small pocket behind it, once you carry the notes that open its stair.

## Returning players

Your existing save carries over. Return to **Behind the Stone in Reedfen**, beyond the first amber door at Old Stone, to find the tuft of down you need for the updrafts. A save that already earned the down keeps it.

## Also in this release

- New finding, pickup and stone voices for the rose note, with the same tuning as the carried hum.
- Pickup and ring cues stay audible while a music moment plays.
- The sky's haze eases back after a music moment, and the stone glyphs sit centered in their notches.
