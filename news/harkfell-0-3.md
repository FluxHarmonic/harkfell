Harkfell 0.3 opens up a new region underground: the Pipes! It's also the first version you can install and play offline through your web browser.

## The Pipes

You've probably heard the wind sounding a note behind the fallen rock at the Old Cut. Carry the amber note from Reedfen there, and the way finally opens.

Under the Old Cut, someone long ago bored the limestone into pipes. Some hang from the ceiling, others rise out of the floor, and the paths wind between their mouths. Wind crosses holes far above you, and the whole cave answers in slow, low breaths. It's 24 rooms, which brings the world to 82.

It's dark down there, as dark as the glass caves. The moths that roost in the Pipes keep their distance at first, but they come close to the right note, and once they do, they light the way for you.

Watch out for the static. In a few places the old pipes have cracked, and a crackle of pale light hangs between the broken lips. You'll hear it before you reach it, and if you touch it, it sends you back a little way.

Keep an eye on the walls, too. The people who carved the Pipes left pictures behind, and they have a story to tell about the Hollow.

At the very bottom of the last pipe, a cold blue note is waiting. Bring it home and see what it does!

## Light

The covered parts of Reedfen are a little darker now, and the fireflies there gather around you when you carry the amber note, lighting the reeds as you go.

## Also in this release

- You can install Harkfell from your browser and play offline after the first load.
- The start screen quietly tells you when an update is ready and what's new after you reload.
- Entering and leaving water each splash once again; treading at the surface stays quiet.
