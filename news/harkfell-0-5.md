Harkfell 0.5 takes you deeper than you've been before: two new regions beneath the ones you know, the Underhum and Wrongwater!

## The Underhum

You've walked past the heart stone in the Hollow many times. Once you're carrying all four notes, amber, green, blue and rose, it finally answers, and the way below opens.

Under the Hollow, old carved passages run deep into the fell, and everything hums: a low sound you feel as much as hear, with stones that ring when you pass and chimes that seem to be listening for something. Look for the Gallery of Stones, and for the shelf where the bearers rested.

If you want to go further, a dry cistern holds an optional climb down to the very last step. The Underhum is 13 rooms.

## Wrongwater

Somewhere beneath the Ice Well in Glasswood, the water stopped running the right way. Lakes hang overhead, a waterfall pours upward, rain rises out of the puddles, and the icicles grow toward the ceiling. The way in is hidden under a drip that rises instead of falling, and some routes mean swimming between pools that hang in the air.

There's a seventh note down there, a violet one, in the Drowned Grove. Carry it back up and listen: it adds a darker, quieter voice to the music in the regions you already know. There's also a resting place behind a wall that isn't quite a wall. Wrongwater is 12 rooms, and when you're done, there's a way out into the Pipes.

Together the two regions add 25 rooms, which brings the world to 129. Your 0.4 save carries over.

## New music

Both regions have their own music and sounds: the Underhum's hum, its stones and its listening chimes, and Wrongwater's rising drip, Rain Rising and the Seventh's Rest. I also rebuilt the Pipes' music around a brighter stone organ, so its ambient music, its musical moments and the blue note's pickup now share one theme.

## What's next

Harkfell's finale is coming in 0.6.

- The Linux version no longer crashes when you press Escape to close the game.
- In the Pipes, the lighting on the way home, the moths' glow, the opening markers and the electrical sparks are improved.
- The installed web version keeps its identity, hides the fullscreen button when you're already fullscreen, and keeps fullscreen available in desktop app windows.
